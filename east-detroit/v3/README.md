## Housing case research: read before proposals

**[Home Forward and Detroit: two housing institutions, overlapping work](housing_authority_case_home_forward_vs_detroit.md)** documents the real organizational patterns, operating failures, financial evidence, federal constraints, and potential coordination choices. It supplies direct links for every case finding. The instructor has established a **City and County Housing Division** and a separate **East Detroit Regional Housing Authority**. Their coexistence is a scenario fact, not a student decision. No housing application or integration plan is preselected.

## Baseline in one paragraph

By 2030 East Detroit has **382,417 residents** in **155,800 occupied households**, with **20 provisional neighborhoods** across a *conceptual* **115.4-square-mile** municipality, most of which is not densely built. It is a newly consolidated city-county in a Phoenix-like growth corridor east of Denver International Airport. Students establish the basis, plan implementation and negotiate how internal city-county offices and independent partners will work together. No technology stack, governance board, first service, contract, or agency data exchange has been adopted.

## What is real and what is fictional

**Actual references:** the Colorado Air and Space Port area, roads, municipal boundaries, current Adams County and Watkins Census figures, Colorado's state-supervised/county-administered benefits, and institutional comparisons from Michigan and elsewhere. **Fictional exercise:** East Detroit itself, its census and districts, city sports franchises and facilities, its constitutional creation, and any service or policy that students approve in the exercise. **Unresolved:** neighborhood polygon geometry, formal charter, public-finance commitments, outside-agency participation and system design.

## Cultural anchors in the fictional city

The Hangar at Space Center is a 17,500-seat venue for the Denver Fawns (WNBA) and Colorado Apex (PWHL). The Denver Pilots (Triple-A, fictionally relocated from Albuquerque) play at Aerotropolis Park (16,210 seats). EDPAC has a 2,200-seat hall, and the East Detroit Health System operates a Level I trauma center. None of these facts settles a service integration or funding agreement.

## Files and editing

Markdown files are working documents. Proposed class decisions go in the shared decision register; the instructor can promote adopted language to the dossier while preserving who made each decision. The scenario GeoJSON contains a **provisional** boundary and 20 neighborhood **label points only**. A mapping agent must overlay real GIS sources, draw neighborhood polygons, and report conflicts before declaring a map complete. Do not use the earlier `../east_detroit_scenario.geojson` as the new boundary.

The previous map package in the parent directory remains for historical reference. A map agent's rebuilt live map should be linked here when reviewed and published.

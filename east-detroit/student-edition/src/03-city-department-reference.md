# Municipal departments

East Detroit's city-county government carries out both local and county functions. The entries below describe the offices, the kinds of records involved in their work and the external laws or operating models that shape them.

## D-01 Mayor and County Executive's Office

Directs municipal administration, appoints officials where the charter provides, and presents executive policies and spending proposals. Council appropriations and independent agency boards remain separate sources of authority.

**Records and operations:** Executive correspondence, appointments, administrative orders and budget proposals.

**Read:** [Colorado Local Government Handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook), [TABOR overview](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/brief-overview-taxpayers-bill-rights-revenue-limit).

## D-02 City-County Council and Budget Committee

Passes ordinances and appropriations, reviews executive proposals and holds public hearings. Tax and debt powers operate under Colorado constitutional limits, while the city clerk maintains legislative records.

**Records and operations:** Ordinances, hearings, approved budgets, contracts requiring legislative approval and audit findings.

**Read:** [Colorado Local Government Handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook), [TABOR overview](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/brief-overview-taxpayers-bill-rights-revenue-limit).

## D-03 City Manager and Administration

Coordinates departments, staffing and routine operations under delegated authority. A manager may direct internal workflow but cannot bind an independent district without an agreement or delegated power.

**Records and operations:** Agency plans, work orders, staffing plans, service metrics and administrative directives.

**Read:** [Colorado Local Government Handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook), [Colorado public records](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/colorado-law-summary-colorado-open-records-act-cora).

## D-04 Human Services

Delivers county-level food, cash and family services under Colorado's state-supervised system. Eligibility, verification and fair hearings follow state and federal program requirements; they are not set by a city application form.

**Records and operations:** Applications, case notes, program notices, benefit determinations and appeal records.

**Read:** [Colorado human services memorandum](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/colorados-human-services-delivery-structure-0), [CDHS county services](https://cdhs.nxt-dev.colorado.gov/our-partners/counties).

## D-05 City Housing Division

Administers local housing policy, development and preservation finance, homelessness prevention and city-funded rent or homeowner assistance. It is separate from the regional housing authority that administers its own federal assistance and properties.

**Records and operations:** Local assistance applications, development contracts, grants, property agreements and payments.

**Read:** [Detroit Housing and Revitalization](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/housing-and-revitalization-department/about-hrd), [Home Forward authority profile](https://www.apps.homeforward.org/faq-about-us/).

## D-06 Planning, Building and Land Use

Prepares land-use plans, administers zoning and coordinates building and development review. Airport operations, drainage, water service and existing road access affect where new housing and industry can be located.

**Records and operations:** Parcel zoning, permit applications, inspections, development agreements and comprehensive plans.

**Read:** [County land-use statute](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-30/county-planning-and-building-codes/article-28/part-1/section-30-28-111/), [Air and Space Port land-use plan](https://adamscountyco.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/space-port-subarea-plan.pdf).

## D-07 Address Office and GIS

Maintains official geographic references for roads, parcels, addresses and service areas. Postal addresses, school zones, property tax records and emergency-dispatch locations do not always describe the same thing.

**Records and operations:** Street address registry, parcel boundaries, GIS layers, address corrections and map history.

**Read:** [Adams County GIS data](https://data-adcogov.opendata.arcgis.com/), [Colorado local government](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook).

## D-08 Finance, Tax, Assessor and Procurement

Prepares financial reports, assesses property, manages purchasing and payments, and reports expenditure and revenue obligations. Property valuation, appropriations, tax collection and contract award involve different official roles.

**Records and operations:** Assessment rolls, budgets, invoices, contracts, procurement files, expenditures and audit records.

**Read:** [TABOR guide](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/brief-overview-taxpayers-bill-rights-revenue-limit), [Colorado public records](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/colorado-law-summary-colorado-open-records-act-cora).

## D-09 Digital Service and Records

Operates municipal applications, technical security, access permissions and records practices. A common portal does not replace each department's duty to correct its own source records or authorize data use.

**Records and operations:** Service logs, system accounts, software contracts, data agreements, retention schedules and incident records.

**Read:** [Estonia's X-tee operations](https://www.ria.ee/en/state-information-system/data-exchange-platforms/data-exchange-layer-x-tee), [ADA requirements](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/).

## D-10 Police, 911 and Emergency Management

Coordinates police response, dispatch and emergency planning. Police and emergency call records are governed by different rules from routine municipal records; accurate location data depends on address systems and mapping.

**Records and operations:** Dispatch events, incident records, emergency contacts and response plans.

**Read:** [Criminal Justice Records Act](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_24-72-301), [Next Generation 911](https://www.911.gov/issues/next-generation-911/).

## D-11 Fire Department

Provides fire suppression, rescue, prevention and first response. Colorado law allows fire protection districts to undertake ambulance and rescue services, but local agreements and adopted fire codes determine the East Detroit division of duties.

**Records and operations:** Incident reports, inspection records, fire plans and station response maps.

**Read:** [Fire protection district powers](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_32-1-1002), [Colorado local government](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook).

## D-12 Public Health

Maintains community health assessment and health-protection functions under the local board of health. Colorado law requires county or district public health agencies and assigns duties such as inspection, disease monitoring and a periodic public health plan.

**Records and operations:** Public health reports, inspections, communicable disease information and community health plans.

**Read:** [Local public health law](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_25-1-506), [HIPAA Privacy Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html).

## D-13 Parks, Recreation and Cultural Affairs

Runs public recreational facilities, parks programs and cultural assets. Residency fees, facility booking, public contracts and accessible public participation are administrative matters with public records obligations.

**Records and operations:** Facility inventories, park reservations, concession agreements and program registrations.

**Read:** [Colorado Local Government Handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook), [ADA requirements](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/).

## D-14 Transportation and Public Works

Maintains local streets, drainage, snow operations and public works. Regional bus and rail schedules and fares belong to RTD; city street projects and regional service require coordination.

**Records and operations:** Road asset records, permits, street closures, drainage maps and capital project schedules.

**Read:** [Colorado Local Government Handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook), [RTD board and responsibilities](https://www.rtd-denver.com/about-rtd/board-office).

## D-15 Clerk, Civil Rights and Appeals

Keeps official municipal proceedings, public records processes and access to local service review. The records custodian evaluates CORA requests; program-specific benefit appeals and court cases use their own legal processes.

**Records and operations:** Council minutes, ordinances, records requests, complaints, notices and administrative decisions.

**Read:** [Colorado Open Records Act](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/colorado-law-summary-colorado-open-records-act-cora), [ADA requirements](https://www.ada.gov/resources/2024-03-08-web-rule/).

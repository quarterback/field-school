# Regional and independent public bodies

Each organization below serves some or all of East Detroit while maintaining a governing body or program authority separate from the city-county administration. These arrangements are part of the city's public service landscape.

## A-01 East Detroit Unified School District

An independent school district governs public schools, enrollment and school operations through its own board. The city provides roads, addresses and other services, but school attendance, student records and placement follow the district's duties and federal and Colorado student privacy law.

**Records and operations:** Student enrollment, attendance, school transport, discipline and education records.

**Read:** [FERPA regulations](https://studentprivacy.ed.gov/ferpa), [Colorado student privacy](https://ed.cde.state.co.us/dataprivacyandsecurity).

## A-02 East Detroit Public Library District

The library district has its own board and revenue arrangements. It manages libraries, patron accounts and library programs; Colorado protects records identifying which materials and services a person has used.

**Records and operations:** Borrowing and patron records, library cards, program registration and facility use.

**Read:** [Colorado library patron privacy law](https://law.justia.com/codes/colorado/title-24/libraries/article-90/part-1/section-24-90-119/), [Colorado local government handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook).

## A-03 East Detroit Water and Sewer Authority

The water and wastewater authority manages its own facilities, revenue, service connections and capital capacity. Colorado water and sanitation districts have distinct powers. Detroit's separation between the local water department and the Great Lakes Water Authority is a relevant operating comparison.

**Records and operations:** Utility accounts, meter readings, connections, service maps, capital plans and debts.

**Read:** [Colorado water districts](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/sites/default/files/water_handbook_redesign_k.pdf), [Detroit's local water department](https://detroitmi.gov/departments/water-and-sewerage-department).

## A-04 Regional Transportation District (RTD)

RTD plans and operates regional transit through a fifteen-member elected board. It controls schedules, fare policy, operations and rider data. Public schedule formats differ from information tied to a particular rider.

**Records and operations:** Routes, schedules, fare transactions, performance reports and vehicle locations.

**Read:** [RTD board office](https://www.rtd-denver.com/about-rtd/board-office), [RTD transparency and operations](https://www.rtd-denver.com/open-records).

## A-05 State of Colorado Human Services and State IT

Colorado supervises county-delivered human services. State agencies own statewide program rules, system access and appeal responsibilities. County offices carry out eligible work, using systems and procedures established with the state.

**Records and operations:** State program policy, system access rules, eligibility records and appeals.

**Read:** [Colorado human services delivery memorandum](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/colorados-human-services-delivery-structure-0), [County human services partnership](https://cdhs.nxt-dev.colorado.gov/our-partners/counties).

## A-06 East Detroit Regional Housing Authority

An independent public housing authority serves East Detroit and neighboring participating communities. Its commission governs its budget and operations; federal assistance uses HUD rules and funding. Portland's Home Forward and Detroit's Housing Commission demonstrate different governance and performance pressures.

**Records and operations:** Voucher files, inspections, property leases, waitlists, payment records and federal reports.

**Read:** [Home Forward's authority and jurisdiction](https://www.apps.homeforward.org/faq-about-us/), [HUD income verification](https://www.hud.gov/helping-americans/public-indian-housing-eiv).

## A-07 East Detroit County Court

The county court handles trial matters within its jurisdiction, including eviction proceedings and smaller civil cases. It is funded locally under East Detroit's charter and follows the Denver county court precedent; judicial case decisions and records are separate from city executive processes.

**Records and operations:** Case filings, hearing calendars, judgments, orders, records of service and court payments.

**Read:** [Colorado courts: Denver](https://www.coloradojudicial.gov/courts/trial-courts/denver-district), [Colorado local government handbook](https://content.leg.colorado.gov/publications/2023-local-government-handbook).

## A-08 East Detroit Health and Hospital Authority

The independent public hospital authority operates the city's safety-net hospital. Its board and operating duties follow Colorado's Denver Health model. Medical charts and clinical disclosures are governed by healthcare privacy law and hospital policy.

**Records and operations:** Patient charts, claims, admissions, service contracts and clinical operations.

**Read:** [Colorado hospital authority statute](https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_25-29-103), [Medical record privacy](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html).

## A-09 East Detroit Health System

A separate Level I trauma and teaching hospital provides specialized clinical services and flight medicine. It coordinates with public safety, public health and insurers, but keeps its own healthcare records and obligations.

**Records and operations:** Clinical charts, referrals, emergency-care records and medical billing.

**Read:** [HIPAA Privacy Rule](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/index.html), [Denver Health authority history](https://www.denverhealth.org/news/2017/01/denver-health-and-hospital-authority-20th-anniversary).

## 2. Withdrawn premature commitments

The uploaded V2 dossier contains ten departmental records phrased as adopted rules: ED-Core ID and ED-Wallet; three-phase platform rollout; automatic school seats; provisional GeoIDs with a 48-hour limit; automatic housing income checks and subsidies; auto-granted parks access with telemetry; emergency 60-minute tokens; RTD wallet fares; historic preservation auto-holds; digital public-art voting and library digital onboarding. It also implies 100% voucher uptake, uniform student assignment, and “zero burden” outcomes without implementation evidence.

**V3 explicitly treats every one of these as an option to be decided, modified or rejected by the class.** The sports and cultural institutions are setting facts, not proof that technology or fee policies have been adopted. The original governance packet's completed procurement, public cloud choice, proposed board membership and predetermined recreation release are likewise open.

## 3. The three-part project boundary

**A. Establish the basis:** document jurisdiction, neighborhoods, service responsibilities, population, assets, resident problems, legal/program limits, and a baseline of how services currently work within the fiction.

**B. Plan implementation:** departments propose rules, governance and buying choices, staffing, costs, technology, first service, information flows, review rights, and demonstrable results.

**C. Coordinate stakeholders:** internal city-county departments must participate and can contest details; independent school districts, RTD, water authorities, state/federal bodies, neighboring governments, hospitals, employers and community organizations may consent, refuse or negotiate. The plan adapts accordingly.

## 4. Michigan comparison without quietly overriding Colorado law

The new comparative governance packet keeps Detroit regional water authority, neighborhood digital stewards, Michigan charter-county power transfer, and other institutional examples as **precedents**. Colorado's actual arrangement is the default legal setting. When the class wants a Michigan-inspired practice that Colorado does not already authorize, the exercise must identify the hypothetical enabling rule and affected actor. A preference for “better” rules becomes a documented choice, not a fabricated legal statement.

## 5. Geographic truth labels

- **Real:** CASP study area and roads, real agency jurisdiction and statutes, actual Census/ACS baselines, actual DEN-Adams airport agreements.
- **Scenario:** 2030 population, city-county existence, 20 neighborhoods, facilities and sports teams, fictional growth history, invented housing and income metrics.
- **Open:** precise county boundary, adopted neighborhood polygons, local charter and representation, service choices, water financing, external partnerships, data handling, technology and procurement.

The old PNG was a **schematic illustration** and should be treated as superseded. The prior GeoJSON is retained for traceability but not for future neighborhood generation.

# The setting

East Detroit has 382,417 residents across twenty provisional neighborhoods. It combines city services and county duties in one fictional government, but many of the organizations residents deal with are independent of it. Growth brings new parcels, crowded schools, expensive housing, long commutes, income gaps, and demand for water, safety, recreation, permits and benefits.

# Who holds authority

City-county departments must take part in the spec. Every other public body can join a shared arrangement, join on its own terms, or refuse.

# What the city-county can and cannot require

A city-county can assign duties to its own departments through its charter, ordinances, administrative orders and approved budgets. It cannot control statewide eligibility rules, federal programs, independent school boards, private records, RTD, nearby municipalities or separately constituted authorities.

- Internal departments must show up, make a proposal and account for carrying it out.
- External parties may decline and state their terms.
- If a department refuses to promise something unlawful or unfunded, record the issue and who can resolve it.
- Keep program appeals and legal review separate from technical administration.

# Colorado: the legal setting

Colorado law governs East Detroit. Where real law would require state action, write "fictional enabling rule needed." Do not claim a local vote overrides state law.

## Consolidated city and county

Broomfield is a real Colorado city that gained county status through a state constitutional amendment. East Detroit was created by a similar fictional measure. That measure set up no particular board, software platform, elected structure or service priority. A consolidated government has fewer handoffs between city and county services. It still cannot override external authorities.

## County-administered benefits

Colorado's human services are state-supervised and county-administered. For programs such as food assistance, county offices deliver services under state and federal rules. So East Detroit has operating duties an ordinary city lacks. Program policy, appeals, privacy rules and access to state systems still apply.

## Intergovernmental cooperation

Colorado's constitution lets governments cooperate and contract to provide functions each may legally provide. That does not allow taking an independent agency's information without its consent.

## A city-county court

Each Colorado county normally has a state-funded county court for smaller matters: evictions, small claims, civil cases up to \$25,000, misdemeanors, traffic and protection orders. A state district court hears larger cases. Denver, a consolidated city and county like East Detroit, is the exception. Denver, not the state, funds its county court, and Denver appoints its judges. The Colorado Constitution has a section specific to Denver's county judges (Article VI, §26).

SCENARIO: The East Detroit County Court follows the Denver model. No other Colorado county has this arrangement, so East Detroit's founding amendment includes a fictional enabling rule for it. Felonies, family and probate cases go to an ordinary state district court. The city-county pays for the court, but its judges decide cases independently and it controls its own records. So this course treats it as an outside agency.

## A public hospital authority

In 1997, Denver's public hospital left city government and became the Denver Health and Hospital Authority. Under Colorado law it is a political subdivision that is not an agency of the state or the city. The mayor appoints its eleven-member board, subject to city council confirmation.

SCENARIO: The East Detroit Health and Hospital Authority, a public safety-net hospital, follows this model. It is separate from the East Detroit Health System, the trauma and teaching hospital.

## A library district

SCENARIO: The East Detroit Public Library District runs the libraries. It is an independent district with its own board and tax levy, not a city department. The Boulder Public Library District (Table 2) is a real Colorado library district governed separately from its city.

## The Denver airport precedent

Denver International Airport sits on land that used to be in Adams County. Legal authorization, elections and agreements governed the annexation and the economic arrangements that followed. East Detroit faces the same questions next to another jurisdiction's facility: who pays, who owns the land, who collects revenue and who approves new uses.

# Michigan: comparisons, not law

A Michigan arrangement applies only if the class writes and adopts it and says where Colorado law or the fictional enabling measure would allow it.

## Charter-county functions

Michigan's Charter Counties Act lists functions a charter county may take on, including public health, police, fire, welfare, land use, recreation and utilities, and lets certain city functions move to the county by agreement. You could adopt something similar as a fictional East Detroit charter clause. A transferred function still needs the affected government's consent where the law requires it.

## Detroit's water arrangement

The Great Lakes Water Authority (GLWA) leases and operates Detroit's regional water and sewer facilities. The Detroit Water and Sewerage Department owns the city's local system and serves its customers. Ownership, operation and customer service can sit with different bodies. That split is one model for East Detroit's Water and Sewer Authority; any arrangement is OPEN.

## Neighborhood technology stewards

Detroit's Equitable Internet Initiative built community-run networks. Trained Digital Stewards maintained them, giving residents paid roles and real responsibility beyond surveys.

You may propose mixed board appointments, an elected commission, a neighborhood council, a contracted regional operator or an internal city department. The decision record must say who holds each decision right and where that power comes from in the scenario.

# Housing

The City Housing Division (EDHD) is a city-county department. The East Detroit Regional Housing Authority (EDRHA) is independent and serves East Detroit and nearby participating communities. The pair is modeled on Detroit (a city housing department beside an independent housing commission) and Portland (a city housing bureau beside Home Forward). A local rent-relief grant and a federal housing voucher are separate obligations. Not every overlap between them is waste. Reading 3 covers both cases; any agreement between EDHD and EDRHA is OPEN.

# Rules for using Colorado and Michigan arrangements

1. **Colorado fact.** Start from real Colorado responsibilities and the fictional city-county's valid public powers.
2. **Michigan precedent.** Say what the comparison arrangement does and why it might work better.
3. **Fictional enabling change.** If an arrangement can't simply be adopted in Colorado, write the authorizing clause or the needed state partnership into the simulation. A student vote does not amend real Colorado law.
4. **Conflicts.** Record the options, the right or interest affected, who can settle the conflict and any outside party that must consent. Pick a rule and say why. The "better" state's rule does not win automatically.
5. **Independent parties.** They can refuse, and a city-county resolution does not count as their consent.
6. **Federal and state program boundaries.** City policy can't get around them. Label any fictional waiver or pilot as fictional and have the right simulated authority approve it.
